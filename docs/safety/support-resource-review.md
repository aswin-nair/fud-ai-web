# Support-resource review

Last verified: 2026-08-20  
Next required review: 2026-11-20, and before every release candidate

The app lists staffed human support and professional directories, not a retired
brand helpline or an AI substitute. Numbers and service details were checked
against the organizations' own current pages:

| Region | Resource | Verified details |
|---|---|---|
| United States | [National Alliance for Eating Disorders](https://www.allianceforeatingdisorders.com/find-treatment/) | 866-662-1235; licensed eating-disorder therapists; Monday–Friday, 9am–7pm ET |
| England | [Beat](https://www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk/get-information-and-support/get-help-for-myself/support-now/) | 0808 801 0677; phone/webchat Monday–Friday, 3pm–8pm; separate Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland lines are on the page |
| Canada | [NEDIC](https://nedic.ca/hey-there/) | 1-866-633-4220; trained human staff via phone, chat, or email; users should check the page for current channel hours |
| Australia | [Butterfly Foundation](https://butterfly.org.au/get-support/helpline/) | 1800 33 4673; qualified counsellors; 8am–midnight AET, seven days |
| Other countries | [Academy for Eating Disorders directory](https://community.aedweb.org/expert-directory) | searchable international professional directory |

The product must not imply these services are emergency care. Immediate-danger
copy continues to direct people to their local emergency number and, in the
United States, 988. A reviewer must repeat the source checks, update both clients
and this record together, and attach the review to the release evidence ledger.
