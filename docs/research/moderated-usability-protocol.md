# Moderated activation study

Recruit five to eight adults who are actively trying to build a logging habit.
Do not recruit minors or ask participants to disclose diagnoses, precise body
measurements, photos, API keys, or other sensitive data to the moderator.

## Tasks

1. Explain what the product is in the participant's own words after the welcome.
2. Complete the adult age gate and a synthetic profile supplied by the study.
3. Interpret a deliberately adjusted safe target explanation.
4. Log a real or synthetic first meal without moderator assistance.
5. Repeat a meal using recent/favorite and edit a serving.
6. Recover from an AI/network failure using manual logging.
7. Pause tracking, locate support, export data, and explain deletion behavior.

## Capture

- eligible onboarding completion and first-log completion;
- time and taps for each log method;
- abandonment point and assistance required;
- comprehension of estimates, safety adjustment, cloud/offline state, export,
  deletion, and BYOK storage;
- accessibility or emotional-safety concerns in the participant's words.

## Exit criteria

- at least 80% of eligible participants finish onboarding and a first log without
  help;
- no critical safety, privacy, or comprehension defect;
- p75 standard logging time meets the hardware protocol;
- every observed P0/P1 defect is fixed and re-tested before beta.

Store consent, raw recordings, and notes outside the source repository under an
approved retention policy. Commit only aggregate, de-identified findings.
