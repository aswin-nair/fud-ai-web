# Tuckori — proposed brand direction

Status: concept for approval. The application and Momo have not been renamed or changed.

## Name

**Tuckori** — pronounced **tuck-or-ee**.

A food-linked name inspired by “tuck in,” with a friendly, ownable-sounding ending. This is a proposed coined brand, not a claim that the word has never been used. Avoid inventing a foreign-language meaning or acronym for it.

Positioning: a personal food journal with useful calorie and macro tracking and a lively food buddy. Broad enough for everyday meals, not only snacks or weight loss. The main brand does not need an “AI” suffix; AI can describe relevant features.

Working tagline: **Your plate. Your pace.**

Brand relationship: **Tuckori is the app. Momo is your food buddy.**

Example introduction: “Welcome to Tuckori. I'm Momo. You bring the food; I'll bring the commentary.”

One drawback: “tuck in” is more familiar in some English-speaking markets than others. Pair the name with a clear descriptor, **Food & calorie journal**, on initial contact and app-store listings.

## Logo direction

- A custom, chunky lowercase `tuckori` wordmark: confident, warm, legible, slightly unconventional.
- A compact food-linked `t` symbol that works independently in an app icon. No second mascot or face; Momo remains unchanged.
- Flat tomato red, cream paper, and ink black; egg-yolk yellow as an accent. Strong contours, limited detail, no glossy effects.
- Starting palette: Tomato `#E74735`, Cream `#FFF7E8`, Ink `#24221F`, Yolk `#FFD951`.
- Deliver the initial artwork as a logo concept. After selection, refine/rebuild the approved geometry into a small-size-tested SVG, monochrome lockup, transparent exports and favicon set. Generated raster artwork is not a production vector master.

## Preliminary name screening — 10 September 2026

Queried the exact name, food/app/calorie combinations, search-engine-indexed Apple/Google store pages, and nearby spellings `Tuckory`/`Tukkori`. No obvious exact-name food-tracking application surfaced in this limited screen. It is not proof of availability or a comprehensive app-store search. Historical near-name uses exist.

Some rejected directions had clear current uses:

- [Nomlet](https://nomlet.app/) already uses that name for a meal-related application.
- [Morsio](https://www.morsio.app/) is already a food-passport application.
- [Bitefolk](https://bitefolk.com/) is already a recipe brand.
- [Dabbly](https://dabbly.app/) is already an application brand.
- [Noshly](https://noshly.io/about) already operates in food; that also weakens a near-spelling such as Noshli.
- [Historical Tuckory reference](https://catalogo.beniculturali.it/detail/PhotographicHeritage/2000156849) is a reminder that this is not a claim of absolute lexical uniqueness.

Domain registration, social handles, trademark registries, similar-sounding marks, and local-language checks remain unverified. Do not buy a domain, claim legal exclusivity, or replace production branding on the strength of this initial screen alone.

Prompt: “Create a professional, original logo presentation for Tuckori, a playful personal food and calorie journal whose existing mascot stays unchanged. Render the exact lowercase word tuckori. Custom chunky soft-edged typography, confident adult-friendly food-club identity, food-linked abstract t symbol; no new face or character. Flat tomato #E74735, cream #FFF7E8, ink #24221F and yolk #FFD951. A large clean primary horizontal logo on cream occupies the upper area; below, show the same icon in a tomato rounded-square app tile and the same lockup in one-color ink, with generous separation. The mark suggests a lowercase t with a subtle spoon-like curved foot and a small bitten detail on the crossbar, using a strong compact silhouette, not a pile of utensils. Original geometry, excellent optical spacing. No gradients, no 3D, no mockup objects, no shadows inside the mark, no mascots, no extra slogans, no watermarks. Landscape design board, restrained layout, crisp vector-like edges. All wordmarks must read tuckori exactly, t-u-c-k-o-r-i.”
