WHERE THIS BREAKS
Paint cost
Four shadows per surface, times every card, inside a scrolling list, on a cheap Android. Budgeted before there are two hundred of them.
Contrast
Inset shadows darken exactly where text sits, and it accumulates with elevation. Small muted text on a deep surface is the worst case.
